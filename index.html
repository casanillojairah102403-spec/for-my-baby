Straight from the heart

Hi, my pretty babyy.

I don't really know where to start, but I wanted to make this for you because some things are just easier to write than to say out loud.

I want you to know how genuinely happy I am that I met you. The more I get to know you, the more I appreciate the little things about you—your personality, the way you think, the way you handle things, and even the random moments we share. I didn't expect you to become someone this important to me, but somehow, you did.

And baby, I hope you know that you don't always have to be okay around me. You can have bad days, feel overwhelmed, be quiet, or simply not know what you're feeling. You don't have to hide those parts of yourself.

I know you sometimes think na baka mapagod ako sa'yo, or baka iwan kita because of certain things about you. But please don't let those thoughts convince you of that. I care about you, not just when everything is good, but even when things aren't.

I want you to know that I'm always here. Even on the days na sa tingin mo mahirap ka intindihin, I'll still try. I may not always have the right words, but I'll listen, I'll stay, and I'll always try to understand you.

You don't have to be perfect, baby. Just be yourself. Yung good sides mo, yung bad days mo, yung random and silly parts mo—lahat 'yan kasama sa kung sino ka.

So whenever you feel like you're too much, I hope you come back to this and remember that you are cared for, appreciated, and important to me.

I'm really glad I met you, my pretty babyy. And I'm even happier that I get to know you a little more every day.
